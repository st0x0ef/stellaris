# FAQ

You have questions? We have answers!

### Why can't I eat on planets?
- Players can't eat on planets without oxygen. To solve this problem, you need to use the Vacuumator to put food into a can.


### This mod looks like another one... Are you stealing a mod?
- Nope! We don't steal other people's mods! Project Stellaris has been created by the maintainers of Beyond Earth and reuses assets from this mod. The creators of Ad Astra also used assets from Beyond Earth, so it's normal if these two mods look the same!


### Will this mod be available for this Minecraft version?
- This mod will be ported to future Minecraft versions but won't be backported to an older version than `1.21.1`.
