# Rocket

Want to go to space and explore new planets? Well you first need a rocket!

## Crafting the rocket

First, you will need to craft the `Rocket Station`.

![Rocket Station craft](/recipes/rocket_station.png)

Then, you will be able to craft the Rocket.

![Fuel Refinery](/recipes/rocket.png)

::: warning
There is only one recipe for the `Rocket`. You will be able to modify it with upgrades
:::

## Place your rocket

To place your rocket, you need 9 `Rocket Launch Pad` blocks to create this structure:

![Launch Pads](/screens/launch-pad.png)

Then, you just need to right-click on the launch pads with your `Rocket`.

## Starting your rocket

To start your rocket, you need to input some fuel in the top-left slot. Then, when the Fuel Tank is full, just press the space bar!


## Upgrades

Want to pimp your rocket or explore planets even farther? It's possible with `Rocket Upgrade`!

There are four types of Rocket Upgrades:

- **Motor Upgrade**: This upgrade changes the fuel needed by the Rocket
- **Tank Upgrade**: This upgrade changes the maximum capacity of the Rocket fuel tank
- **Model Upgrade**: This upgrade changes the model of the rocket
- **Skin Upgrade**: This upgrade changes the skin of the rocket

![Launch Pads](/screens/rocket-pimp.png)

To apply these upgrades, enter the rocket, open your inventory and place the upgrade in the right slot (it's one of the four slots at the bottom)

![Launch Pads](/screens/rocket.png)

Some planets are far away and need a big amount of Fuel. This can be achieved with the `Tank Upgrade`.
