# Fuel

In Project Stellaris, one of the main resources is the Fuel. It allows you to use the Rocket and the JetSuit.

## Get Oil
There are two ways to find oil.


## Oil Finder
To get Oil, you will need to craft the oil finder.

![Oil finder](/recipes/oil_finder.png)

With this item, you can right-click on a block and it will tell you if there is oil in the chunk.

When you finally find oil in a chunk, you can craft the `pumpjack drill` and then you will be able to craft the `pumpjack`.

![pumpjack drill](/recipes/pumpjack_drill.png)

![pumpjack](/recipes/pumpjack.png)

::: warning
When placing the `pumpjack`, you won't be able to get the pumpjack drill! Make sure to place it in a good place.
:::

When powered, the pumpjack will drain the oil in the chunk. Then, you will be able to get back the oil in buckets!

## Craft the Fuel Refinery

Now, you need to transform the oil to fuel. This is possible because of the Fuel Refinery Block.

![Fuel Refinery](/recipes/fuel_refinery.png)

::: warning
The `Fuel Refinery` block needs to be powered.
:::

## Usage

If Oil is present in the first tank and if the block is powered, the oil will be transferred into Fuel.

The top-right slot has two functions:
- When putting a bucket, the bucket will be filled with Fuel
- When putting a JetSuit Chestplate, the JetSuit Chestplate will be filled with fuel

![Fuel Refinery](/menus/fuel_refinery.png)
